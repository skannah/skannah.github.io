# Research Interests

My work focuses on autonomy for safety‑critical robotic systems, with emphasis on perception, planning, and control under uncertainty.

---

## Safety‑Critical Autonomy

- Robust guidance, navigation, and control (GNC)
- Lyapunov‑based safety layers for docking and approach
- Fault‑tolerant autonomy for mission‑critical environments

---

## Perception & Dense Reconstruction

- Multi‑camera dense 3D perception
- Stereo depth estimation and reconstruction
- Semantic + geometric fusion for navigation

---

## Uncertainty‑Aware Decision Making

- Risk‑bounded planning
- Belief‑space control
- Probabilistic tracking and multi‑hypothesis data association

---

## Multi‑Sensor Fusion

- EKF‑based fusion pipelines
- JPDA/MHT tracking for dynamic environments
- IMU + vision + LiDAR fusion for navigation

---

## Embedded Machine Learning

- Real‑time inference on constrained hardware
- Model compression and optimization
- Deployment of CV/ML models in robotics systems
